# AI Course Outline: Working with AI

This document outlines the structure and learning objectives for the course "Working with AI: A Practical Guide."

## Course Goal

To demystify Artificial Intelligence and empower non-technical professionals and students to use it as a practical, everyday tool for work and study.

---

## Modules

### Module 1: What is AI, Really?
-   **Goal:** Build a useful and non-intimidating mental model of how Generative AI works.
-   **Hook:** Debunking the common "magic brain" misconception of AI.
-   **Core Lesson:** AI as "autocomplete on steroids"—a powerful pattern-matching engine, not a thinking machine.
-   **Empowering Takeaway:** The user's role is to be a good director, not a technical expert.

### Module 2: The Art of the Prompt
-   **Goal:** Teach the most critical skill for getting useful results from any AI model.
-   **Hook:** A "Garbage In, Garbage Out" comparison showing how a vague prompt fails and an effective prompt succeeds.
-   **Core Lesson:** The CARE framework for structuring prompts: **C**ontext, **A**sk, **R**ules, **E**xamples.
-   **Empowering Takeaway:** Good prompting is a communication skill, not a technical one.

### Module 3: Common Use Cases
-   **Goal:** Showcase the most practical, high-value tasks you can accomplish with AI today.
-   **Hook:** Framing AI as a new assistant who can handle tedious information-based tasks.
-   **Core Lesson:** Hands-on exercises for four key applications:
    1.  **Summarization**
    2.  **Brainstorming & Ideation**
    3.  **Structuring & Organizing**
    4.  **Drafting & Rewriting**
-   **Empowering Takeaway:** Use AI to handle the first 80% of routine tasks to free up time for high-value strategic work.

### Module 4: Thinking with AI
-   **Goal:** Move learners from giving single commands to using AI as a collaborative thinking partner.
-   **Hook:** The "smart-but-dumb intern" analogy—an AI has knowledge but no common sense, requiring guidance.
-   **Core Lesson:** Using **Iterative Prompting** and "think step-by-step" to tackle complex problems as a conversation with the AI.
-   **Empowering Takeaway:** AI can be used to tackle complex, creative, and strategic problems, with the user steering the process.

### Module 5: The "Sharp Edges"
-   **Goal:** Teach responsible, safe, and critical AI usage.
-   **Hook:** A real-world cautionary tale of an AI "hallucination" causing a major professional problem.
-   **Core Lesson:** Understanding and mitigating the three key risks:
    1.  **Hallucinations** (making things up)
    2.  **Bias** (reflecting and amplifying stereotypes)
    3.  **Privacy** (handling of confidential information)
-   **Empowering Takeaway:** The user is always responsible and must cultivate a mindset of healthy skepticism.

### Module 6: The Future is a Process
-   **Goal:** Recap the course and empower learners to continue learning as the technology evolves.
-   **Hook:** The observation that AI tools are changing rapidly, making a flexible mindset more important than mastering any single tool.
-   **Core Lesson:** A full recap of the key skills and mental models from the course.
-   **Empowering Takeaway:** A simple, actionable plan for continued learning: Start Small, Get Curious, and Stay Grounded.
